    Privacy & data

    Cycle records are stored with your account data under users/{uid} and are readable only by you (existing Firestore security rules). They are never sent to AI services or analytics.

    The reminder uses the next-date estimate from your own recorded history (it is not a certainty or medical advice). It only appears while Pro Habit Tracker is open in this browser — a website cannot run after it is closed.

    Turning off hides the feature but keeps your records so you can restore them. Deleting removes every cycle entry permanently.

    GPS activity tracker
